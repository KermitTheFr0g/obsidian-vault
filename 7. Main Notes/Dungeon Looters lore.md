# Overview
This document is an outline for the Lore and the ideas that I want to present across [[Dungeon Looters]].

# The Cosmic Array
This is where the universe of Dungeon Looters is set. To begin with this is going to be a single large planet, but I want to expand this out to further planets in the future. Leaning on the side of Magic Technology instead of space age kind of technology.

The characters and races aren't going to have advanced technology and I want that theme to stay throughout and for all of the characters. I like the idea of some of the races using an advanced Magic Technology but it is still with primitive weapons. For example some sort of magic shield or melee weapon.

# Races
### Dragon worshipping cult
A select few members are able to wield the magic of a dragon, fire breathing etc.

### The Dragons
 The Dragon's that the cult worship themselves - these are a myth but do exist and will talk and pass messages to the leaders of the cult.

### Pirates with flying ships
They steal and will sell and buy anything they can get their hands on to make money. The ships have propellers on the side of the ship to move through the sky.

### Mining Goblins
One leader goblin with an entire community that just mines - they live inside of a mountain. They sell and horde massive amounts of ores.

### The Dark Knights
This is a kingdom of dark and gloomy knights wearing thorny black armour. Wielding huge swords and are hunting the dragons to steal their power.
They want to take out the [[#Dragon worshipping cult]] and take their power from them.

### Magic Kingdom
Focus on automation, farming and want peace but they have strong warriors to keep this peace and will take out factions that are stepping out of line.
Top of their castle has a huge staff that acts as a turret and can shoot a powerful beam. 
Prisoners are kept in an hour glass, different substance inside to depend on the prisoner. [[Short story - insert name Prison]]

### Travelling Dwarves
They move from location to location, focus on trading, but they not only trade goods but humans too.

### The Advanced Magic Technology Race
I am not sure how this looks - but a race that has been gifted magic technology from a higher power. Although it is still primitive tools they are powered with something that even themselves do not understand. Portals, powerful shields, swords with a powerful edge, and other weapons like that.
