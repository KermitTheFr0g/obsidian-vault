# Notes
- A user owns a guild
- A guild can have multiple hunters, but only 5 active at one time.

- Quests randomly spawn throughout the Cosmic Array every 3 hours. All of the old quests that were available if there is no users currently completing the quest they are deleted.
- Quests reward the player from a random pool of items, this pool of items can be found by getting the details of a quest before embarking one of your hunters on it. 
- Quests reward both the User and User's Hunter with XP upon completing.
- If a User attempts a Quest which is too difficult for their Hunter there will be a chance 

- Taverns are populated with Hunters to be hired at midday with between 1 and 5 hunters. All of the old hunters which could have been hired here are removed for this new fresh pool.

- NPC's stock and currency is reset everyday at midnight. Similar to how the Tavern works their stock is reset with a new random roll of items which could be stocked.
- For an NPC some NPCs will show an item as 0 quantity always, this means that they will buy the item but they won't ever stock it themselves. (There are plans to expand this idea in the future).

- Different regions have different specialties, one for mining, another for foraging, another for fishing. And then the fighting quests will be spread out across these with various different awards.
- A user can only see Quests that they have active Hunters in the same location as. However, they if they have another hunter in the same region they can complete that quest without needing to be in the same location.
- A user can only see Taverns that they have an active Hunter in the same location as. They can only access a Tavern when they have an active Hunter in the same location too.
- A user can only see an NPC that they have an active Hunter in the same location as. They can only access an NPC when they have an active Hunter in the same location too.

# General Rules
## User
A User owns a Guild and all of their Hunters and Gold are part of that Guild.

While playing Dungeon Looters you will need to remember your Moral compass. Different Quests or actions will either raise or lower your Moral compass, this is going to both open and close interactions with Hunters and NPCs and with deeper plans for this mechanic in the future!
## Hunters
A Guild can own as many Hunters as they would like. However, they are only able to have 5 Hunters active at any point. This means that you need to be careful about where you position your Hunters across the map, and how you want to distribute tasks and Quests to the Hunters.

As your Hunter levels up they will add another level to each of their stats every level. Equipping gear to your Hunter will also increase their stats making them more powerful and able to tackle high level Quests more quickly.
## Quests
Quests randomly spawn throughout the map every 3 hours. At this 3 hour mark all old Quests that were available are removed, if you are currently on a Quest you are able to complete it and won't be affected. New Quests appearing throughout the map is to encourage players to move around and explore different locations and create a sense of scarcity for Quests because once a player has embarked on a Quest it disappears from the map for all players.

To find a Quest you need to have a Hunter in the same Location (this is the specific Location not Region), however you can have any of your Hunters within the same Region as the Quest embark on the Quest. So finding the Quest is the hardest thing to begin with. This is why travelling between locations is encouraged so much.

It is always advised to calculate how long a Quest will take and the completion chance. This is to ensure your Hunter isn't stuck in a Quest for a longer period of time than intended and has a high chance of completion so you aren't wasting your time. But always remember there is a sense of urgency here because you're competing against other players for this Quest.

Upon completing a Quest the player is rewarded with a pool of items, each having their own drop rate from the Quest. These items can be found within the details of the Quest before embarking. Both the User and the User's Hunter are rewarded with XP upon completing.

Don't forget if your Hunter doesn't complete the Quest because the completion chance is too low and you get unlucky you don't get anything! In the future there will be further punishment and your Hunter will be put on cooldown because they are injured.
## Taverns
Taverns are where Hunters looking to be hired will be hanging out. Similar to Quests you will need to be in the same Location to be able to find a Tavern, however you will need to be within the same Location to view inside of the Tavern too.

Hunters can be hired for a price! They will only cost Gold, however some Hunters will only be hired if you have either enough negative or positive Moral compass. Taverns will be refreshed with new Hunters at midday everyday removing all of the current Hunters and creating a new fresh pool.

It's important to note that once a Hunter has been hired by another player they will no longer show in the Tavern so again, you need to be quick with hiring a Hunter if someone powerful appears in a Tavern.

Different Hunters with stats to match their Region are also more likely to spawn in their specific Region, for example a Mining Hunter is more likely to spawn in the Mining Region.
## NPCs
NPCs are where you can trade your hard earned Gold and loot in Dungeon Looters. NPCs mimic the same mechanic as Taverns and you will only be able to see an NPC if you are in the same Location as them, and you will only be able to interact with them if you are in the same Location as them too.

NPCs are restocked every day at midnight, restocking their items and their Gold allowing you to sell more items to them. All the NPCs have a set stock but will randomise through this stock, some days having more powerful items and other days not. 

Their buy and sell prices also differ with items depending on the Region that an NPC is in, so it will be beneficial to check how much items are with different Vendors because you could buy in one Region and then sell back in another Region.

When an NPC is showing they always have 0 quantity of an item this is how their buy price for something is set. 

Similar to the Taverns, some NPCs might only trade with you if you have enough positive or negative Moral compass.
## Regions
The map is split out in to Regions, the names of these Regions and their specialties are subject to change. However, the game design is that each Region will be focusing on one specialty than another. You should use this to your advantage when finding your niche of Quest you would like to corner. Creating a powerful Guild of Hunters to capitalise on one type of Quest and sell these rewards in another Region that lacks in that specific resource.
## Additional Notes
When searching for Quests, Taverns, and NPCs it's important to note that only your Active hunters will be looking. Any of your Hunters that are Benched even if they are in same location as a Quest for example, they will not be searching so you will not see the Quest appear.
# General Progression Flow
The idea is that the game can be played however the user would like. However, the general flow for a Guild to become more powerful is to:
- Move your hunters around the map to find Quests
- Complete quests that are in line with your Hunter's level
- Receive items and gold from completing a quest
- Gear up your Hunter for harder quests
- Hire new Hunters from Taverns
- Trade items with NPCs to make more gold and hire more experiences Hunters
- Continue this cycle, but it's important to find a niche that you want to gear your Hunters towards so they are more efficient at completing those Quests.

Remember this is a game about optimisation and creating the best routing and cornering your section of the market so that you can become the most powerful.
